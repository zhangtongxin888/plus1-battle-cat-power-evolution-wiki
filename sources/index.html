Editorial policy

How claims enter this guide

  • Official developer text and Roblox APIs are preferred for game facts.
  • Third-party sources are used cautiously for time-sensitive codes and interface routes.
  • Conflicts are disclosed instead of smoothed over.
  • Strategy suggestions are labeled as guide tips, not hidden mechanics.
  • Exact enemy names, costs, multipliers, formulas, and world counts stay unpublished until verified.
