Quick answers

Know before you grind.

What does clicking do?

Every click gives Evolution Power, according to the official game description.

How do I earn Wins?

Defeat enemies. The official description directly names Wins as the reward for enemy victories.

Is World 2 real?

Yes. Roblox has an enabled badge named Reached world 2! The unlock requirement is not confirmed publicly.
